Learning outcomes

  • Estimate \(R_t\) from incidence data with EpiEstim, and say which assumptions drive the answer.

09:00 · Lecture 4

Space and individuals

  • From compartments to individuals: Gillespie simulation, and cellular automata on a grid.
  • Local transmission: infections can only happen at the edge of the epidemic. Cumulative cases grow like \(t^2\) (in 2D), not \(e^{rt}\).
  • Spatial structure within hosts: tumours, biofilms, drug gradients and evolutionary rescue in structured populations (Tomasini & Peischl).

Models meet data

  • The renewal equation: incidence today = \(R_t \times\) (past incidence weighted by the serial interval).

  • Other data sources: deaths and hospitalisations, sequences (phylodynamics, birth–death skyline).
